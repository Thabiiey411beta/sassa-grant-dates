Terms & Disclaimer

Not SASSA

This is an independent informational website. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the South African Social Security Agency (SASSA), the Department of Social Development or the South African Government. It does not use SASSA’s logo or branding. Nothing on this site is an official SASSA communication.

Information only — not advice

Everything on this site is general information, not legal, financial or tax advice, and not a determination of anyone’s entitlement to a grant. Only SASSA can decide whether you qualify for a grant, how much you receive and when it is paid. The SRD eligibility self-check and the household calculator are guides only — SASSA makes the real decision.

Accuracy

We check every payment date and grant amount against SASSA and Department of Social Development announcements, and we date every check. But dates and amounts can change without notice, and mistakes can happen. Always confirm a payment date or amount on the official SASSA website (sassa.gov.za or srd.sassa.gov.za) or by calling SASSA toll-free on 0800 601 011 before you travel or make plans. We are not liable for any loss caused by relying on information on this site.

What this site cannot do

This site cannot check your grant status, change your banking details, approve or decline an application, lodge an appeal or pay a grant. Anyone claiming to do those things on our behalf is not us. Never pay anyone who offers to speed up your grant, and never share your PIN, password or bank details with anyone claiming to act for SASSA.

Use of this site

You may use this site for personal, non-commercial purposes. Do not copy our pages wholesale, do not present our content as your own, and do not use this site in a way that misleads people into thinking it is SASSA or a government service.

Advertising

This site may show third-party advertising in future. Advertisements are not endorsements, and we do not control their content. We do not accept payment to change a payment date or grant amount.
