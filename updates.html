Every official announcement behind the dates and amounts on this site, newest first. If SASSA changes a date, the correction appears here the same day — and the date on the calendar changes with it.

Conflicts we did not hide

Two things other guides disagree about, and what we did:

  • SRD end date — March 2027 or March 2028? Both are true at different levels: the Budget funded SRD to 31 March 2027, and the October 2026 mini-budget announced a further year to 31 March 2028, subject to formal steps. We say March 2028 with that condition stated, and we will update this log when the regulations are published.
  • June 2026 children’s grants — 4 or 5 June? The official DSD schedule says 4 June 2026. One guide lists 5 June, which is the “exceptions” day in SASSA’s schedule, not the children’s payment day. We follow the official schedule: 4 June.
  • SASSA WhatsApp number. SASSA’s own October 2026 statement gives the self-service WhatsApp line as 082 601 0111. Some guides list a different number (082 054 0016). We use the number SASSA itself published.

Sources for every entry: Department of Social Development, SASSA media statements, National Treasury Budget 2026 and Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement, October 2026.
