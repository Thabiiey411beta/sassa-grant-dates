Grant not paid or under review?

Work through these steps in order. Most problems are one of four things: it is not your payment day yet, your bank details are wrong, your grant is under review, or your application was declined.

1. Check the date first

Make sure it is actually your payment day. See the payment calendar — older persons are paid first, then disability, then children’s grants. The SRD grant is paid in batches in the last week of the month, not on the three main payment days. If it is not your day yet, wait. If it is your day and the money is not there by the next morning, keep going.

2. Check your bank details

For the SRD grant, wrong or unverified bank details are one of the commonest reasons for a missed payment. On the official portal (srd.sassa.gov.za), check that the bank account is in your name and that the details are correct. If you changed banks, update the details there — do not give your bank details to anyone on WhatsApp, Facebook or SMS.

3. What “under review” means

SASSA regularly reviews grants to check that the person still qualifies — for example, that income, employment or other circumstances have not changed. If your grant is under review, SASSA should send you a notice telling you what it needs from you. Respond to that notice. If you ignore it, your grant can be suspended. In November 2026, grants under review were paid on 5 November, separately from the three main payment days.

If you were not told why your grant is under review, phone SASSA toll-free on 0800 601 011 or visit your nearest SASSA office with your ID.

4. The Postbank Black Card

SASSA’s old gold cards are being replaced by Postbank Black Cards. If your gold card stops working or is declined at an ATM or till, you need a Black Card. You can get one at Postbank branches and selected retailers — take your ID with you. You can also ask SASSA to pay your grant into your own bank account instead, so you do not depend on the card at all.

5. How to appeal a declined SRD grant

If your SRD application was declined for a month, you can appeal to the Independent Tribunal for Social Assistance Appeals. The tribunal is separate from SASSA — SASSA does not decide its own appeals.

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za/appeals.
  2. Enter your ID number and the cellphone number on your application, then the SMS PIN.
  3. Choose the declined month. You must appeal each declined month separately — appealing one month does not appeal the others.
  4. Submit and keep the confirmation. The tribunal aims to finish an appeal within 60 to 90 days.

Deadline: 90 days from the decision. Appeal as soon as you can — SASSA suggests within 30 days. Appealing is free. Do not pay an agent to appeal for you.

For a declined permanent grant (older persons, disability, child support), appeal at your SASSA office or directly to the tribunal — the online SRD appeals portal is only for SRD.

6. At a pay point or ATM

  • Do not queue on the first payment day if you can avoid it — the money stays in your account.
  • Never share your PIN with anyone, including someone offering to “help” at an ATM.
  • If an ATM keeps your card or the money does not come out, report it to the bank immediately and keep the slip or reference number.
  • If a pay point says your grant is not there, check your status first (see how to check your status) before travelling again.
Scam warning. Nobody can speed up your payment, unblock your grant or get you approved faster — not for a fee, not on WhatsApp, not on Facebook. SASSA never asks for payment, your PIN or your password. If someone offers to fix your grant for money, walk away and report the number to SASSA on 0800 601 011.
