SASSA payment dates 2026/27

SASSA pays in the same order every month: older persons first, disability grants the next payment day, children’s grants after that. The SRD R370 grant is not on this calendar — it is paid in batches in the last week of each month, and SASSA announces its dates month by month.

Last checked 26 November 2026. Source: Department of Social Development 2026/27 payment schedule (approved by National Treasury, 20 March 2026) and SASSA media statement, 28 October 2026.

November 2026 was different — and only November. Because of the local government elections on 4 November 2026, SASSA moved that month’s payments earlier: older persons on Friday 30 October, disability on Monday 2 November, children’s grants on Tuesday 3 November. Grants under review were paid on 5 November. SASSA confirmed on 28 October 2026 that this was a once-off change and that December 2026 to March 2027 stay exactly as first announced.

What about the SRD R370 grant?

The SRD grant does not follow the three-day calendar above. SASSA processes SRD payments in batches during the last week of each month, and the dates change every month. For November 2026, SASSA paid SRD on 23 and 25 November. December 2026 SRD dates have not been announced yet — we will add them here the day SASSA announces them. Your own SRD payment date is shown on the official portal: srd.sassa.gov.za/sc19/status.

Do I have to collect on the payment day?

No. Once the money is in your account it stays there. You do not lose it if you do not go on the first day, and the queues are worst on payment days. If your grant is paid into a bank account or Postbank Black Card, you can draw it whenever you are ready, and you can use the card to buy goods without drawing cash.

Why do the dates move sometimes?

The schedule is set for the whole financial year (April to March) and approved by National Treasury. A date moves only if something forces it — a public holiday, or in November 2026, the local government elections. When SASSA changes a date, we flag it as Revised here and note the change in the update log the same day.
