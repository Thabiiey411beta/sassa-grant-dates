Household grant calculator

Set how many people in your household get each grant, and see the total per month.

Amounts: April 2026 rates (Foster Child R1,300 from 1 October 2026). Last checked 26 November 2026.

How this works

  • Use the + and − buttons to set how many people in your household get each grant. Two grandparents on the Older Persons Grant? Set it to 2.
  • Child Support Top-up — only for children who have lost one or both parents. It is R280 on top of the R580 Child Support Grant, so R860 per child in total. Count the top-up separately from the child support grant itself.
  • The total updates as you tap. It is only an estimate — SASSA pays each grant separately, and your household total is just the sum of the grants you receive.
Private by design. This calculator runs entirely in your browser. We do not ask for your name, ID number or phone number, and nothing you tick or type is sent to us or stored anywhere.

Not sure whether you qualify for the SRD grant? Take the SRD eligibility self-check. Wondering when the money arrives? See the payment dates.
