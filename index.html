SASSA payment dates: December 2026

Your grant is paid over three days at the start of the month — older persons first, then disability, then children’s grants. The money stays in your account, so there is no need to queue on the first day.

Quick answers

How do I check my SRD status?

On the official SRD portal at srd.sassa.gov.za/sc19/status, or dial *120*3210#, or WhatsApp “Hi” to 082 601 0111. Full steps: How to check your status on the official site.

My grant was not paid. What do I do?

First check that it is actually your payment day and that your bank details are correct. Then see Grant not paid or under review — it covers reviews, banking details, the Postbank Black Card and how to appeal.

Could I get the SRD R370 grant?

Take the two-minute SRD eligibility self-check. It asks five questions and tells you whether you may qualify. We do not ask for your ID, phone number or any personal details.

How much does my household get in total?

Use the household grant calculator — tick the grants in your household and it adds them up. Nothing you enter leaves your phone or computer.
