Contact us

If you spot a wrong date or amount on this site, or have a question about the site itself, email us at thabangzulu94@gmail.com. Corrections to dates and amounts are our first priority — tell us what is wrong and where you saw the correct figure, and we will check it against SASSA’s announcements.

Please do not send us your ID number, phone number, bank details or grant reference number. We cannot check your status or fix your grant, and email is not a safe place for those details. If you need help with your grant, contact SASSA directly — see below.

For help with your grant, contact SASSA

  • Toll-free: 0800 601 011
  • SRD website: srd.sassa.gov.za (apply, check status, update banking details, appeal)
  • SASSA website: sassa.gov.za
  • WhatsApp self-service: 082 601 0111 (send “Hi”)
  • USSD: *120*3210#
  • In person: your nearest SASSA local office, with your ID

We cannot answer grant questions on SASSA’s behalf, and we cannot speed up a payment or an appeal.
