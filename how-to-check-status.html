How to check your SASSA SRD status

Only SASSA can tell you your status. This page shows you the official ways to check — we cannot check it for you, and neither can any other website.

Before you start: protect yourself. Only enter your ID number and cellphone number on the official SASSA website (srd.sassa.gov.za) or the official USSD and WhatsApp channels below. SASSA will never ask you to pay money, share your PIN or password, or send your bank details by SMS or WhatsApp to “speed up” your payment. Anyone who asks for that is running a scam.

Option 1: Online (free, works on any phone)

  1. Go to srd.sassa.gov.za/sc19/status in your browser.
  2. Enter your South African ID number and the cellphone number you used when you applied.
  3. Follow the steps on screen. It will show your status for each month — approved, pending or declined — and your payment date if one has been set.

Use the same site, srd.sassa.gov.za, to apply, update your banking details or check an appeal.

Option 2: USSD (no data needed)

Dial *120*3210# from the cellphone number on your application and follow the menu. This works on any phone, even without airtime for data, and it is free.

Option 3: WhatsApp

Save 082 601 0111 and send “Hi” on WhatsApp. This is SASSA’s self-service line: it can tell you whether your grant is under review and help with other services. Other WhatsApp numbers circulate online — if a number is not the one SASSA itself publishes, do not send it your details.

Option 4: Phone or visit an office

Call SASSA toll-free on 0800 601 011, or visit your nearest SASSA local office with your ID. Office staff can check your status and help with applications and appeals.

What the status means

StatusWhat it means
ApprovedYou qualify for that month. Your payment date will show once SASSA has processed the payment batch.
PendingSASSA is still checking your details. Check again in a few days — do not re-apply.
DeclinedSASSA decided you do not qualify for that month, and it shows a reason. You can appeal — see Grant not paid or under review.
Bank details pending / ReferredSASSA needs your correct bank details or more information. Update them on the official portal.

What about permanent grants (older persons, disability, child support)?

There is no online status check for permanent grants. If you applied at a SASSA office and have not heard back, phone 0800 601 011 or visit the office where you applied, with your ID and your application receipt if you have one. SASSA aims to process a permanent grant application within 90 days.
