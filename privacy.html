Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What we collect: almost nothing

This website does not ask for your name, ID number, phone number, bank details or any other personal information. There is no sign-up, no account and no contact form on this site. If you email us (see Contact), we receive only what you choose to put in that email — please do not send ID numbers, bank details or grant reference numbers.

Calculators and the SRD self-check

The household grant calculator and the SRD eligibility self-check run entirely in your web browser, on your own device. Your answers are not sent to us, not stored on our servers and not shared with anyone. Closing the page erases them.

What our hosting and analytics may record

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information when you visit — such as the pages you visit, your approximate location from your IP address, your browser type and the time of your visit — for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this to identify you, and we do not sell it.

Advertising and cookies

This site may in future show advertising served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor’s use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, although parts of the site may then work less smoothly.

We do not control the ads shown, and we do not receive personal information about you from advertisers.

Links to other websites

This site links to official SASSA and government websites. Their privacy practices are their own — please read their policies when you use them, and only enter personal details on official government sites.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
