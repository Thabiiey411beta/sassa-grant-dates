About this site

This site is built and maintained by Thabang Zulu, an independent publisher in South Africa. It exists for one reason: millions of South Africans depend on a SASSA grant, and the first question every month is the same — when is my grant paid, and how much is it? That answer should take ten seconds to find, not ten minutes of scrolling through a long article.

What this site is — and is not

This site is an independent guide. It publishes SASSA’s payment dates and grant amounts, explains how to check your status on the official site, and helps you work out what your household receives and whether you may qualify for the SRD grant.

This site is not SASSA. It is not affiliated with the South African Social Security Agency, the Department of Social Development or the South African Government. It cannot check your grant status, change your banking details, approve an application or pay a grant. It does not use SASSA’s logo or branding.

How we check our facts

  • Payment dates come from the Department of Social Development’s published 2026/27 schedule, approved by National Treasury on 20 March 2026.
  • Changes come from SASSA’s own media statements — for example, the 28 October 2026 statement confirming the November 2026 dates were a once-off change.
  • Amounts come from Budget 2026 and the Department of Social Development’s adjustment notices.
  • Every figure on the site carries a “last checked” date. When SASSA announces a change, we correct the site the same day and note it in the update log.
  • Where official sources conflict or a date has not been announced yet, we say so instead of guessing. A wrong payment date can send someone to a pay point on the wrong day — we do not take that lightly.

How the site makes money

This site is free to use, with no sign-up and no paywall. In future it may show advertising through Google AdSense. Advertising never affects the dates or amounts shown, and we do not accept payment to change a figure or to promote any loan, credit or “fast-track grant” offer.
