How much is each SASSA grant?

These are the monthly amounts paid from April 2026. They were set in Budget 2026 and apply for the whole 2026/27 financial year, except for the Foster Child Grant, which rose again on 1 October 2026.

Last checked 26 November 2026. Sources: Budget 2026 grant increases (Department of Social Development) and SASSA announcements.

What changed in April 2026?

The April 2026 increases, compared with the 2025/26 amounts:

GrantBefore (2025/26)From April 2026
Older Persons, Disability, Care DependencyR2,320R2,400 (+R80)
Older Persons (75+), War VeteransR2,340R2,420 (+R80)
Foster ChildR1,250R1,290 (+R40), then R1,300 from 1 Oct 2026 (+R10)
Child Support, Grant-in-AidR560R580 (+R20)
SRDR370R370 (no change)

The Foster Child Grant is the only one with a second step: it went from R1,290 to R1,300 on 1 October 2026. If your October payment still showed R1,290, check with SASSA — the R1,300 rate applies from the October 2026 payment cycle.

Child Support Grant Top-up — R280 extra

If you care for a child who has lost one or both parents, you may qualify for the Child Support Grant Top-up of R280 on top of the R580 Child Support Grant — R860 per child in total. It is paid with the children’s grants each month. Ask at your nearest SASSA office whether you qualify; you will need the child’s death certificate(s) and proof that you are the caregiver.

Will grants go up again in April 2027?

Grant amounts are set once a year in the national Budget, usually announced in February and paid from 1 April. We do not guess next year’s amounts. When the 2027 Budget is announced, we will update this page the same day and note it in the update log.

How long will the SRD grant last?

The SRD grant pays R370 a month. It was funded until 31 March 2027 under the February 2026 Budget. In the October 2026 mini-budget, National Treasury announced it will continue until 31 March 2028, subject to the formal steps being completed. The amount stays R370. There is no increase announced for SRD.
