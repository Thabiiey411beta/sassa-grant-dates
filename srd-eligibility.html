Do I qualify for the SRD R370 grant?

Five quick questions. It takes about two minutes, and it is only a guide — SASSA makes the real decision when you apply.

Question 1 of 5

No personal details. We do not ask for your name, ID number, phone number or bank details, and your answers are not sent or stored anywhere. Any site that asks for those details to “check if you qualify” is not to be trusted.

What the SRD grant is

The Social Relief of Distress (SRD) grant pays R370 a month to unemployed adults aged 18 to 60 who have little or no income and do not get any other social grant, UIF money or NSFAS funding. SASSA checks your bank account — if R624 or more a month goes into it, you do not pass the income test. The grant was funded until 31 March 2027, and in the October 2026 mini-budget the government announced it will continue until 31 March 2028, subject to the formal steps being completed.

If you may qualify

Apply on the official site, srd.sassa.gov.za. Applying is free. You will need your ID number and a cellphone number you can receive messages on — but only enter those on the official SASSA site, never on any other website. SASSA checks your application every month, so you can be approved one month and declined the next if your income changes.

If you were declined

You can appeal. See Grant not paid or under review for the appeal steps — you have 90 days from the decision, and you must appeal each declined month separately.
